As initial conditions I have 300 children numbered 1 to 300, 3 trays numbered 1 to 3, 300 bread portions numbered 1 to 300, 300 content portions numbered 1 to 300, and 300 sandwich placeholders numbered 1 to 300. The children assigned to tray 1 are child 1 to 100. The children assigned to tray 2 are child 101 to 200, and the remaining children are assigned to tray 3. Every 3rd child starting from child 1 must receive a gluten-free sandwich, and all other children can receive a regular sandwich. The bread portions whose numbers match the children that need gluten-free sandwiches are gluten-free, and the content portions whose numbers match the children that need gluten-free sandwiches are gluten-free. All trays start in the kitchen, all bread portions and content portions start in the kitchen, and all sandwiches still need to be prepared and served.
My goal is to have each of the 300 children be served a sandwich. For example, child 1 is served a sandwich, child 2 is served a sandwich, child 3 is served a sandwich, and so on.
