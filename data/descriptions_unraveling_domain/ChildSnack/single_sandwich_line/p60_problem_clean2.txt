As initial conditions I have 30 children numbered 1 to 30, 3 trays numbered 1 to 3, 30 bread portions numbered 1 to 30, 30 content portions numbered 1 to 30, and 30 sandwich placeholders numbered 1 to 30. The children assigned to tray 1 are child 26 to 17. The children assigned to tray 2 are child 16 to 7, and the remaining children are assigned to tray 3. Every 3rd child starting from child 1 must receive a gluten-free sandwich, and all other children can receive a regular sandwich. The bread portions whose numbers match the children that need gluten-free sandwiches are gluten-free, and the content portions whose numbers match the children that need gluten-free sandwiches are gluten-free. All trays start in the kitchen, all bread portions and content portions start in the kitchen, and all sandwiches still need to be prepared and served.
My goal is to have each of the 30 children be served a sandwich. For example, child 1 is served a sandwich, child 2 is served a sandwich, child 3 is served a sandwich, and so on.
