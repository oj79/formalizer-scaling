I am moving passengers through a building using elevators. Here are the actions I can do

Move a slow elevator up or down
Move a fast elevator up or down
Board a passenger onto an elevator
Let a passenger leave an elevator

I have the following restrictions on my actions:
I can only move an elevator to a floor that the elevator can reach.
I can only move an elevator up or down between floors that are in the correct above or below relation.
I can only board a passenger if the passenger and the elevator are on the same floor.
I can only board a passenger if the elevator has enough remaining capacity.
Once a passenger boards an elevator, that passenger is no longer waiting on the floor.
I can only let a passenger leave an elevator if that passenger is currently inside the elevator.
Once a passenger leaves an elevator, that passenger is on the elevator's current floor.
Moving elevators has a travel cost that depends on the elevator type and the floors involved.

I have the following travel cost rules:
For any two floors where the destination is above the source by d floors, moving a slow elevator between those floors has travel cost d + 5, and moving a fast elevator between those floors has travel cost 3d + 1.
