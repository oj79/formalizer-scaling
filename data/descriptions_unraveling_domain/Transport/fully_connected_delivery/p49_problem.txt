Packages 1 through 4 are initially at location 1, packages 5 through 7 are initially at location 2, and packages 8 through 10 are initially at location 3. All 3 vehicles have carrying capacity 4, and every road has length 1.
My goal is to have that package 1 is at location 22, package 2 is at location 26, package 3 is at location 21, package 4 is at location 30, package 5 is at location 24, package 6 is at location 28, package 7 is at location 27, package 8 is at location 29, package 9 is at location 25, and package 10 is at location 23.
