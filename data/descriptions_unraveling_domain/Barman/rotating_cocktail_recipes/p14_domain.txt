I am a robot barman preparing drinks. Here are the actions I can do

Grasp a container
Leave a container on the table
Fill a shot glass from a dispenser
Refill a shot glass
Empty a shot glass
Clean a shot glass
Pour a shot into a clean shaker
Pour a shot into a used shaker
Empty the shaker
Clean the shaker
Shake the shaker to make a cocktail
Pour from the shaker into a shot glass

I have the following restrictions on my actions:
I can only grasp a container if it is on the table and the chosen hand is empty.
I can only hold one container in each hand at a time.
I can only fill a shot glass if the shot glass is empty and in the correct state for the action.
I can only pour ingredients into the shaker if the required shot glass is being held.
I can only shake the shaker if the correct two ingredients for a cocktail are already in the shaker.
I can only pour from the shaker into a shot glass if the shaker currently contains the desired beverage and the shot glass is clean and empty.
Shot glasses and the shaker may need to be emptied or cleaned before they can be reused.
