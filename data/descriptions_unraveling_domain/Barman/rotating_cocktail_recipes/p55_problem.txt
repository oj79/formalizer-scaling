As initial conditions I have 6 cocktails numbered 1 to 6, 3 ingredients numbered 1 to 3, 6 shot glasses numbered 1 to 6, and 1 shaker. All ingredient dispensers are available from the start, and the shaker is clean and empty on the table. The two robot hands are both empty. Shot glass 5 and shot glass 6 are empty but dirty on the table, with shot glass 5 previously used for ingredient 2 and shot glass 6 previously used for ingredient 3. All other shot glasses are clean and empty on the table.
The cocktail recipes follow a repeating cyclic pattern of ingredient pairs. For this problem, cocktail 1 requires ingredients 2 and 3, and each subsequent cocktail shifts forward to the next pair in the cycle (2,3), (3,1), (1,2), repeating as needed through cocktail 6.
My goal is to have that shot 1 contains cocktail 1, shot 2 contains cocktail 2, shot 3 contains cocktail 3, shot 4 contains cocktail 4, shot 5 contains cocktail 5, and shot 6 contains cocktail 6.
