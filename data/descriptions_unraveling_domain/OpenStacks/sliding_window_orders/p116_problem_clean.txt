As initial conditions I have 1000 customer orders numbered 1 to 1000, and 12 products numbered 1 to 12. Every order requires exactly 3 products. For this problem, the sliding window starts at product 6. Order 1 requires products 6, 7, and 8; order 2 requires products 7, 8, and 9; order 3 requires products 8, 9, and 10; and in general each next order shifts the required product window forward by one product. When the numbering reaches product 12, it wraps around to product 1 again. All orders are initially waiting, no products have been made yet, and zero stacks are open initially.
My goal is to have all 1000 orders completed. For example, order 1 is completed, order 2 is completed, order 3 is completed, and so on.
